Privacy Policy

Our Commitment to Privacy

At fischcodes.github.io ("the Website"), we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we handle information when you use our Website.

Information We Don't Collect

We want to be clear: we do not collect any personal information. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Personal identification information
  • Contact information
  • Usage data
  • Cookies
  • Location data
  • Device information

Third-Party Services

The Website is hosted on GitHub Pages. While we don't collect data, GitHub may collect basic access logs. For more information, please refer to GitHub's Privacy Statement.

User Interaction

If you choose to interact with us through GitHub Discussions:

  • Any information you share is subject to GitHub's privacy policy
  • We recommend not sharing personal information in public discussions
  • Your interactions on GitHub Discussions are public

Contact & Feedback

For privacy concerns or any other inquiries, please use: GitHub Discussions

Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

Your Rights

Since we don't collect personal data, there is no personal information to access, modify, or delete. You can freely use our service without providing any personal information.
